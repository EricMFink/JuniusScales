## In-text annotations



 <mark class="hltr-yellow">> The infamous "membership clause" of the Smith Act makes it a criminal offense to be a "knowing" member of any organization which "advocates" the violent overthrow of the government. Evidence of spceific _actions_ aimed at revolution is not required for prosecution; _belief_ is the crime. While other sections of the Smith Act have been freely used in the legal assault upon the Communist party, this clause has lain dormant for two decades. Last year it claimed its first victim.</mark> ([Page 1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/JTAMFL9A?page=1&annotation=text-p1x77y550)) 





 <mark class="hltr-yellow">Refers to Scales as "the only American to be imprisoned solely for being a "knowing member" of a revolutionary party", "who renounced that party and resigned from it after [the 1956 Soviet invasion of] Hungary, nearly _five years_ before he entered the penitentiary."</mark> ([Page 1](zotero://open-pdf/library/items/JTAMFL9A?page=1&annotation=text-p1x521y402))
